Notice : Sale of Immovable Properties
Sale of Immovable Properties
The Ramakrishna Order, through its twin organizations the Ramakrishna Math a registered Trust, and Ramakrishna Mission, a registered Society, having its global Headquarters at Belur, Howrah, commonly known as the Belur Math, established by the world mover Swami Vivekananda a century ago, along with its various branch centres scattered all over the world, has been serving the humanity by rendering spiritual, philanthropic, charitable, social and multifarious welfare activities in India and abroad, for the benefit of the people irrespective of caste, creed and religion.
From time to time, disciples and many other religious and philanthropic minded persons have supported the activities of the said twin organisations through various forms of donation. Several immovable properties have been and are, from time to time, donated by some benevolent devotees. The Ramakrishna Math/Ramakrishna Mission, in order to fulfil the objectives for which these donations were made, is interested to sell some of these immovable properties to the interested and eligible person/institution.
Descriptions of the immovable properties for sale are provided hereinbelow and any person/institution, after conducting due diligence and being satisfied about the same, may approach the General Secretary, Ramakrishna Math / Ramakrishna Mission, as the case may be, in the following address by letter or email, mentioning the property or properties by giving the reference of such property from the list hereinbelow.
