Branch Centres:
As of 1 May 2026, the Ramakrishna Math and the Ramakrishna Mission have 296 branch centres all over the world. Of these, 227 centres are in India and the remaining 69 are in 24 other countries (27 in Bangladesh, 15 in the USA, 3 in Brazil, 2 each in Canada, Russia and South Africa, and one each in Argentina, Australia, Fiji, France, Germany, Ireland, Japan, Malaysia, Mauritius, Nepal, Netherlands, New Zealand, Philippines, Singapore, Sri Lanka, Switzerland, UK and Zambia). Further, there are 60 sub-centres (30 within India and 30 outside India) functioning under the above branch centres.
How to get the address and other details of our Branch Centres :
1. Please type the name/place of the centre in the search box.
2. Scroll down and see the centre details in the table.
3. You can click the More Info link to know more about the centre.
Common Religious Activities of all Branch Centres
All branch centres and sub-centres of the Ramakrishna Math and the
Ramakrishna Mission undertake religious activities comprising daily worship,
bhajans, scriptural classes, lectures, retreats, and celebration of the birthdays of Sri
Ramakrishna, Sri Sarada Devi and Swami Vivekananda and other religious
festivals.
